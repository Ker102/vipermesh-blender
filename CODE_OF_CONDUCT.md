# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

We are committed to a constructive, harassment-free community. Be respectful,
specific, and focused on the technical work. Disagreement is welcome; personal
attacks, harassment, discrimination, and deliberate disruption are not.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues, commits, code, and other
contributions that violate this standard. Repeated or severe violations may
result in a temporary or permanent ban.

Report conduct concerns privately through the maintainer contact options on
the GitHub profile for this repository. Reports will be reviewed as promptly
and confidentially as practical.
